About Our College

A community built on care, curiosity, and character.

Star International College Myanmar brings together a focused campus culture and a rigorous academic program. From early foundation years through university-preparatory studies, our teachers know every student's name, strengths, and goals.

We believe learning happens best in a place that feels like home — so we've built small classes, personal mentorship, and a genuinely welcoming environment for every family who joins us.

  • Small class sizes
  • Bilingual instruction
  • University pathway support

Academic Programs

Programs designed for every stage of learning

From foundation years to university preparation, find the path that fits your child.

  • Primary Education

    A joyful foundation in literacy, numeracy, and curiosity for ages 5–10, taught by nurturing homeroom teachers.

  • Secondary Education

    A full academic curriculum for grades 6–10 that builds critical thinking alongside strong subject mastery.

  • English Language Program

    Intensive spoken and written English coursework, including exam preparation for IELTS and Cambridge tests.

  • IT & Computer Studies

    Hands-on training in office software, coding fundamentals, and digital literacy for the modern workplace.

  • Business & Accounting

    Practical grounding in commerce, bookkeeping, and entrepreneurship for students aiming at business careers.

  • University Foundation

    A bridging year of advanced coursework and counseling to prepare graduates for university admission abroad or at home.

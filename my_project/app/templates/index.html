About Our College

Star International College

Star International College, formerly Star Resources Academy was established in May 2005 for the young generation who aspire to build a career in the hospitality and tourism industry.

Our University has grown tremendously in the last decade and we have proudly received accreditation from the Ministry of Hotel and Tourism, Myanmar Tourism Federation (MTF) as well as the National Skill Standard Authority (NSSA) for intensive courses in hotel and tourism industry.

Academic Programs

Programs built for the hospitality industry

From professional management to the professional kitchen, find the path to your career.

  • Postgraduate

    M.B.A in Hospitality Management

    Our MBA programs are designed for professional managers and executives, with the goal of improving and sharpening the industry's managerial skills and interpersonal abilities.

  • Undergraduate

    Bachelor in Hospitality Management

    This degree combines practical experience, industry exposure, and real-world assignments to lay the foundation for a successful global career.

  • Diploma

    Diploma in Food Preparation & Culinary Arts

    This program is designed for catering professionals, these globally recognized qualifications focus on food preparation and patisserie skills to launch a successful kitchen career.

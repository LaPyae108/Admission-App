Our Service & Support

Training, recruitment and internships for students, employers and businesses across Myanmar.

Service 01

On-site training

Practical training delivered at your workplace, for businesses anywhere in Myanmar. We cover roles such as retail store management and hotel front-of-house operations.

Our approach is hands-on and built on current, industry-relevant knowledge, so your team learns in the environment where they will use it.

Service 02

Recruitment

We recruit for hospitality and business operations roles. Candidates are chosen for their ability to work independently, in a team or in management.

Every candidate has completed education and skills development training with us.

Service 03

Internship program

Diploma students complete a supervised industrial internship as part of their course.

Through our network of industry partners, we help each student find a placement that matches their academic and career goals.

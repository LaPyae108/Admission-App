About Us

A hospitality and vocational management school in Yangon, preparing students for careers in hotels, tourism and culinary arts.

What we offer

Hands-on learning, backed by the industry

Star University is a hospitality and vocational management school offering educational opportunities for people who want a career in the service industry.

Our programs pair practical instruction with on-the-job training at companies across Myanmar. Courses are developed with the support of our industry partners, so students learn skills they can apply straight away in the hotel and hospitality sectors.

59,618+

students have graduated from Star over 20 years

Our history

Star International College, formerly Star Resources Academy

The college was established in May 2005 for young people who want to build a career in the hospitality and tourism industry. It has grown tremendously over the past decade.

Today we are proud to be accredited and recognized by:

  • The Ministry of Hotel and Tourism
  • The Myanmar Tourism Federation (MTF)
  • The National Skill Standard Authority (NSSA), for intensive hotel and tourism courses

Mission and vision

What we do, and where we are heading

  • Develop human resources for the tourism and hospitality industry
  • Supply manpower to the service industry
  • Provide technical consulting to hospitality organizations
  • Give employers a wider pool of skilled labor to choose from
  • Support career development for current and future employees
  • Promote sustainable tourism

Why study at Star

Four reasons students choose us

  • Expert faculty

    Our instructors are industry experts who bring real-world experience into the classroom.

  • Industry partnerships

    We work with leading hospitality organizations to open up opportunities and resources for students.

  • Student future

    We help students find full-time jobs and internships. 80% of our graduates land jobs after graduation.

  • Industry-relevant curriculum

    Our curriculum is updated regularly to reflect industry trends and needs.
